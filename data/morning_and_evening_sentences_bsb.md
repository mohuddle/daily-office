# Morning and Evening Sentences (BSB)

Converted from the 1662 Book of Common Prayer opening sentences.

Scripture is the **Berean Standard Bible**. The 1662 wording is kept
under each entry for comparison. Combined or slightly adapted 1662
sentences are given as the BSB verses they draw from, not as a new
paraphrase.

Machine-readable copy: `web/data/sentences.json`. Morning uses the
sentence for that weekday (Sunday first). Evening uses the next sentence
in the list, so the two offices never share one on the same day.

---

## Ezekiel 18:27

**1662.** When the wicked man turneth away from his wickedness that he
hath committed, and doeth that which is lawful and right, he shall save
his soul alive.

**BSB.** But if a wicked man turns from the wickedness he has committed
and does what is just and right, he will save his life.

---

## Psalm 51:3

**1662.** I acknowledge my transgressions, and my sin is ever before me.

**BSB.** For I know my transgressions, and my sin is always before me.

---

## Psalm 51:9

**1662.** Hide thy face from my sins, and blot out all mine iniquities.

**BSB.** Hide Your face from my sins and blot out all my iniquities.

---

## Psalm 51:17

**1662.** The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and a
contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.

**BSB.** The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and a
contrite heart, O God, You will not despise.

---

## Joel 2:13

**1662.** Rend your heart and not your garments, and turn unto the Lord
your God, for he is gracious and merciful, slow to anger and of great
kindness, and repenteth him of the evil.

**BSB.** So rend your hearts and not your garments, and return to the
LORD your God. For He is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger,
abounding in loving devotion. And He relents from sending disaster.

---

## Daniel 9:9–10

**1662.** To the Lord our God belong mercies and forgivenesses, though
we have rebelled against him, neither have we obeyed the voice of the
Lord our God, to walk in his laws which he set before us.

**BSB.** To the Lord our God belong compassion and forgiveness, even
though we have rebelled against Him and have not obeyed the voice of
the LORD our God to walk in His laws, which He set before us through
His servants the prophets.

---

## Jeremiah 10:24; Psalm 6:1

The 1662 sentence weaves two verses together: “O Lord, correct me, but
with judgement; not in thine anger, lest thou bring me to nothing.”

**BSB, Jeremiah 10:24.** Correct me, O LORD, but only with justice—not
in Your anger, or You will bring me to nothing.

**BSB, Psalm 6:1.** O LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger or discipline
me in Your wrath.

*(Psalm 6:1 in the BSB is preceded by the superscription “For the
choirmaster. With stringed instruments, according to Sheminith. A Psalm
of David.” The sentence above is the verse itself.)*
